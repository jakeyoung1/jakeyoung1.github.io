# PRODUCT.md — Jake Young Portfolio

## Register

brand

## Product Purpose

Single-page personal portfolio for Jake Young, entry-level Data Analyst / Business Analyst (B.S. Business Analytics, Saint Mary's College of California, May 2026). The URL goes on his resume, LinkedIn, and every job application. Its one job: convert a recruiter's 45-second click into a phone screen. Secondary job: give hiring managers technical proof (eight shipped projects) that survives scrutiny in an interview.

## Users

- Recruiters and HR screeners at mid-size and large companies (SoftAppDesigns, USC, Cambridge Associates, SpaceX, Palantir, KKR, Hive). Midday, bright office, deciding fast. Skim-read.
- Hiring managers / analytics leads doing the second look. Read project detail, click GitHub and npm links, judge whether the numbers are real.
- Sports analytics orgs (Lakers application already out; teams, Genius Sports, Stats Perform later).

## Brand Voice

Measured, precise, quietly confident. A statistician's voice: every claim carries a number (R² = 0.707, 53 automated tests, ~60% faster delivery, 10+ varsity programs). Jake spent two years as a sports information director and statistician keeping official box scores; the portfolio should feel like it was made by someone who respects a well-kept stat sheet.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS portfolio templates: gradient hero, icon-card grids, "Passionate about data!" copy.
- Dark "analytics dashboard" cosplay: neon charts on black because the field is data.
- Editorial-magazine affectation: italic display serif, drop caps, broadsheet columns. Jake is not a magazine.
- Anything that fabricates: no invented metrics, no inflated titles ("enterprise-grade" is banned from his vocabulary after it cost him interviews).

## Strategic Principles

1. Every number on the page is real and interview-defensible.
2. Skimmable in 45 seconds top to bottom; rewarding for 5 minutes.
3. Loads instantly: static HTML/CSS, no framework, no build step, deployable to GitHub Pages.
4. The design itself demonstrates data literacy (structure, tables, hierarchy), not decoration.
5. Contact and resume download reachable from anywhere on the page.
