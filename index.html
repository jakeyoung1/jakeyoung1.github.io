dockflow

Bike-share operations · SQL and optimization

2026

Measures Citi Bike’s rebalancing problem from 45.7 million trips (September 2025 to August 2026), with a DuckDB pipeline that tracks every station’s running balance of arrivals and departures. An average weekday leaves 4,558 bikes at the wrong station. Only 590 of them are predictable, and those pass an out-of-time test: 92.5% of flagged stations kept drifting the same way the next season. pitchquality

Expected-whiff model · separating stuff from results

2026

Gradient-boosted model of the chance a swing misses, built only from how the pitch leaves the hand. Trained on 2025 Statcast, then tested on 2026, a season it never saw, across 1.27M pitches in total. The first version scored .652 AUC and lost to a pitcher’s own past whiff rate. The missing piece was approach angle: deriving vertical and horizontal approach angles at the plate raised it to .767. The model’s edge over raw results is biggest on small samples, like a reliever with 25 swings, and gone by 150. A 50/50 blend of model and results beats either one alone. Source: github.com/jakeyoung1/pitchquality.

Python · scikit-learn · pandas · Statcast · 45 automated tests

PhaseMap

Soccer phase analyzer · evidence-checked AI

2026

Reads player and ball tracking data at 25 frames per second, splits a match into possession phases, and measures each one: passes, pressure on the passer, defenders bypassed, back-line height, pitch control. Every measurement becomes a numbered fact. Claude writes the tactical breakdown, and every claim in it has to cite those facts. PhaseMap then checks each number and player name against the facts before the claim reaches the report. All four open-play goals in the sample match are broken down, and every checked claim is backed. Source: github.com/jakeyoung1/phasemap.

Python · NumPy · SciPy · Claude API · tracking data
